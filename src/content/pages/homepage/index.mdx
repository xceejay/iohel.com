I am Joel Kofi Amoako, but most people call me <u>Ceejay</u>. I am an AI and software engineer building AI-powered software and helping businesses worldwide integrate AI into their operations.

My work sits at the intersection of backend engineering and AI. I build scalable, AI-driven systems and automate business workflows using tools like **n8n** and **Zapier**. Whether it's an intelligent agent, an automated pipeline, or a full-stack product with AI at its core, I design solutions that ship fast and hold up under real-world load.

I also offer **consultancy for businesses worldwide**, helping teams adopt AI tools, build automation workflows, and architect software that scales. If your business needs to move faster with AI, I can help you get there.

Before moving into AI engineering, I spent over six years building backend systems. Video platforms, payment infrastructure, chat applications, all using Go, Node.js, Python, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker, and Kubernetes across AWS and GCP. That foundation in systems design and reliability is what makes the AI solutions I build production-grade, not just prototypes.

I started early with Unix and Linux, experimenting with countless systems and even building my own distribution. That curiosity still drives how I approach problems today.

Outside of engineering, I like to read, garden, follow new ideas in crypto, and take photos. Photography gives me a way to create something outside of code.
